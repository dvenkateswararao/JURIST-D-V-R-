Areas of practice

Service matters
Departmental enquiries, disciplinary proceedings, suspension, promotion, seniority, pension and retirement benefits.
Labour and employment
Industrial disputes, termination, wages, gratuity and establishment compliance.
Writ petitions
Article 226 petitions before the High Court on service matters, administrative action and departmental orders.
Civil
Suits for declaration, injunction, specific performance and recovery, and execution of decrees.
Consumer disputes
Complaints and appeals under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 before the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (consumer forum) and the State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.
Criminal
Bail, trial defence and quashing petitions under the BNS and BNSS, and under the IPC and CrPC for older cases.
Property and family
Title, partition and possession disputes; matrimonial, maintenance and custody matters.

Courts

District Courts, Guntur

Civil and criminal trials, bail and magistrate proceedings, family court matters, execution and recovery.

High Court of Andhra Pradesh, Amaravati

Article 226 writ petitions, civil and criminal appeals, revisions, and quashing and stay applications.

Consumer Commissions

Complaints and appeals under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, before the District and State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions.
